# Glimpin for Claude

Ask Claude what happened on your construction sites, and it answers from your Ring cameras.

## Use it

Add the plugin, then press Connect on its Glimpin connector and sign in with the email of your Glimpin account. Ask in your own words, for example "what happened on site yesterday?". Claude opens the photos itself and names the camera and time behind every answer.

## Data

Claude reaches your Glimpin account through the connector, which can only read: your sites, camera status, activity and photos. Glimpin receives the requests Claude makes to it (which site, which days) and keeps a record of each one. It does not receive the rest of your conversation.
